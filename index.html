Scheduled open & close countdowns

The clocks use a bundled January 2026–December 2027 schedule, verified against NYSE and TSX holiday calendars. Regular hours are 09:30–16:00 America/New_York. The next-open clock always shows the earliest upcoming open. While a regular session is in progress, a close countdown appears to its left. NYSE early closes (13:00 ET) on the day after Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve (when it is a trading day) are included; TSX has no Black Friday early close, but NYSE is primary for the close clock. Holidays mean the market is not open. The clocks are a bundled calendar, not a live market-status service. Unscheduled closures are not detected. Outside coverage, the schedule is unavailable. Sources checked November 9, 2026.
